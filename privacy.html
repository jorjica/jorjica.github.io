Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

yvid channel tools is a personal tool with a single user: the owner of the YouTube channels it is used on. It runs only on that owner's own computer.

What data it accesses

Through the YouTube Data API, YouTube Analytics API and YouTube Reporting API, and only for the channels its owner signs in with: the channels' videos and their details, comments on those videos, and the channels' analytics and reports. When the owner asks it to, it updates his own videos' details (title, description, tags, thumbnail, captions, visibility) and posts his own comments.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to show the owner his channels' statistics and to manage his own videos. It is stored only on the owner's computer, as local reports and files. The sign-in tokens are stored only on that computer.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with or transferred to anyone. There are no other users, no server, no advertising and no tracking.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local files removes every copy of the data.

YouTube

This tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Through the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen of this app.
